# Lightweight Review

Review once at normal viewing size. Do not run a long checklist.

The result passes when:

- the shown file is the composed output, not the raw model-generated painting;
- the source image's geometry and visible content are unaltered and fill one outer cell; the two outer cells are equal;
- the painting asset has the same aspect ratio as the source but is smaller inside an ivory interpretation cell;
- there are exactly two cells, no caption embedded in either image asset, no gap or divider, and no photo restyling;
- the painting preserves a small set of source-grounded relational anchors while discarding incidental photographic detail;
- the source functions as semantic evidence only: its camera crop, viewpoint, pose, scale, background geometry, and object coordinates have not been carried over by default;
- the painting has one obvious focal subject or relationship, with supporting information deliberately reduced;
- the source was read and a reconstruction treatment was formed before the brushwork board was selected;
- the treatment expresses a source-specific artistic trait, not only a named mood or a more decorative version of the object;
- the painted composition has been rebuilt around that focal relationship; it is not a one-to-one transfer of the source camera view, background geometry, object placement, and subject scale;
- the reconstruction enlarges a source relation—such as concentrated colour within abundance or heaviness against delicacy—rather than merely enlarging or beautifying an isolated object;
- at least one decisive spatial composition property has changed from the source, alongside at least one consequential change in scale, intensity, colour, density, or directional pressure; colour or texture change alone does not count;
- one source-traceable surprise point is visible at ordinary diptych size and makes the core relation more legible;
- the felt relation is larger and more legible than the depicted object; emotion is visible through composition, colour, direction, density, contour, and quiet space—not merely a faithful image with brush texture;
- human and object forms are painted as planes, masses, marks, and relationships rather than as retained camera details;
- marks describe the form and emotional pressure of their region, and the chromatic construction reads as Van Gogh rather than generic classical, medieval, or polished oil paint;
- a Van Gogh-associated cue, when used, is a subordinate translation of a source-grounded relation rather than a copied motif or style token;
- when a meaning-bearing revelation is used, it arises from source evidence, is legible at final diptych size, clarifies the emotional centre, remains secondary, and is echoed by the title when essential;
- the title names a relation already made visible by the painting; it does not supply an interpretation the image fails to carry;
- the caption remains inside the interpretation cell and outside both image assets: for left–right layouts it sits below the smaller painting, aligned to its left edge; for top–bottom layouts it is clockwise immediately to the painting's right, with its first letter aligned to the painting's upper edge.

If it fails, make one targeted repair: rerun composition for a format failure, or regenerate only the painting for literalness, weak focus, weak emotional force, weak semantic fidelity, or weak Van Gogh character. Do not repair a successful layer.
