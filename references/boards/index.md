# Five Felt-World Boards

The boards are silent visual studies from the curated 24-work repertoire. They are secondary references, never templates. Select **exactly one** only after reading the source’s signals and relation with [../perceptual-translation.md](../perceptual-translation.md), and after completing the integrated perceptual reconstruction treatment required by the main Skill.

The selected board contributes one painterly action across hierarchy, colour, marks, scale, and quiet. It is a study of the hand used to realise an already-decided artistic idea. It must never contribute a subject, layout, local coordinates, or recognisable Van Gogh motif. The source photo and reconstruction treatment remain the authorities for identity, meaning, and layout.

Only the five boards named and described below are active references. Other image files that may exist in this directory are candidates or archived studies, not additional boards to select. They become eligible only if they are explicitly added to this index.

## `turbulence.png` — 激荡

Action: organise dispersed space into a directional field pressing against one anchor, path, or resistance.

Study sources: *The Starry Night*, *Wheatfield with Crows*, *Olive Trees*.

## `anxiety.png` — 焦灼

Action: compress or interrupt an unstable relation; let local colour conflict, broken direction, and spatial pressure make the unresolved point visible.

Study sources: *Café Terrace at Night*, *The Night Café*, *Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear*, *The Sower*.

## `stillness.png` — 静谧

Action: hold a stable centre through measured intervals, restrained contrast, and gentle continuing marks, so calm remains alive.

Study sources: *Starry Night Over the Rhône*, *Almond Blossom*, *Wheat Field with Cypresses*, *The Siesta*.

## `hardship.png` — 困顿

Action: give burden, depleted attention, difficult waiting, or endured pressure a bearer in body, hand, object, support, or enclosing field.

Study sources: *The Potato Eaters*, *Portrait of Dr. Gachet*, *At Eternity’s Gate*, *Van Gogh’s Chair*.

## `vitality.png` — 生机

Action: make a living event rise, branch, gather, or spread outward; use expansion and rhythm rather than literal floral imagery.

Study sources: *Sunflowers*, *Irises*, *Still Life: Vase with Irises*, *The Mulberry Tree*.

## Prompt boundary

Describe the board only by its action, for example: “use this only to make burden visible through support, worn material, and enclosing quiet.” Never ask to paint “like the board,” and never use a second board. A second governing world creates a generic hybrid rather than a clear interpretation. If the board's look is more memorable than the source-specific artistic idea, the board is being used too early or too strongly.
